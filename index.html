Document Reader & Viewer

Privacy Policy • Effective Date: September 7, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Document Reader & Viewer, developed by Mecorven ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates and handles user information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Document Reader & Viewer is designed as a local offline document utility. Your documents and personal data stay entirely on your device and are never collected, transmitted, or stored on external servers.

2. Information Collection and Use

Our app does not require account creation, registration, or submission of personal data. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.
  • Local File Processing: All documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Text, and Images) opened within the app are processed strictly on your local device for display purposes.
  • No Network Transmission: The app does not upload or sync your files to any cloud server or remote database.

3. Device Permissions

To provide full document reading and file management functionality, the app requires access to device storage permissions:

  • Storage Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): This permission is required solely to allow the app to scan, list, open, and view document files saved on your device storage.

These permissions are used strictly for local operations initiated directly by you.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Document Reader & Viewer is clean and ad-free. We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytic tracking frameworks, or behavioral profiling tools that capture user metrics.

5. Data Security

Because all data remains local to your device, the security of your files relies on your device's built-in Android operating system security features. We recommend keeping your device updated and secured with a PIN, pattern, or biometric lock.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with a revised effective date.
